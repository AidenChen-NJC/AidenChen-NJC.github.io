When everyone can build, knowing what to build wins

Watching 18-year-olds ship products in a weekend made me feel obsolete. Then I broke down where value actually comes from — and what's left when execution becomes free.

People say we're going to be replaced by AI if we don't know how to make the most out of it.

When I saw some random 18-year-old high school kids vibe-code an entire product in a weekend, I felt obsolete.

Let's deconstruct this.

Value = intent + logic + execution + judgement

AI commoditized execution, which means step 3 is way easier now; everyone should be able to build, as long as they are good with AI.

So the formula becomes intent + logic + AI + judgement, and my fear comes from knowing the fact that I'm falling behind in AI knowledge.

But there are four elements; hence, building is only half the battle. It must be easier for CS/EE students to familiarize themselves with vibe-coding than for those with non-technical backgrounds, but you can be better at identifying a real problem or making judgments about the market.

The world will be flooded with mediocre products.

Your value lies in curated intent.

While others are excited that they can build, you will win by knowing exactly what should be built and why it will succeed in a competitive market.

(I would argue that this is even more important now since the world is only getting noisier.)
