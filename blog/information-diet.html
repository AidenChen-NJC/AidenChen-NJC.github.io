You need an information diet你需要一份資訊節食菜單

Human focus caps at 4 hours a day. I was burning the first hour on X — so I built a pipeline that distills my chosen sources through a VC, a domain expert, and a founder, and ships at noon.人的專注力一天上限只有 4 小時。我卻把第一個小時燒在 X 上——所以我做了一條 pipeline，把我自己挑的 source 經過一位 VC、一位領域專家、一位創辦人的視角蒸餾過，每天中午出刊。

Everyone should have their own information consumption system, just like your diet.

Imagine this.

  • You can only have 6,000 thoughts a day. Even “I want pasta for dinner” counts as 1.
  • You are at your prime for 4 hours a day. The rest of the day, you are only 30% as sharp as you were at your prime.

It’s f-king the sh-t out of me.

Because it’s not imagination. It’s true.

Human focus has a cap.

  • You think ~6,200 independent thoughts a day. (Tseng & Poppenk, Nature Communications, 2020)
  • Peak cognitive performance caps at ~4 hours a day, even for elite performers. Beyond that, returns drop sharply. (Ericsson, 1993)
  • After 3 hours of focused work, miss rates rise 71% and reaction times slow ~5%. (Boksem et al., 2005)
  • Even with 10-min breaks every 50 min, cognitive efficiency doesn’t fully recover after 4.5 hours of rest. (Brazaitis & Satas, 2023)

Dickens. Hemingway. Stephen King. All wrote for only 4 hours a day. The rest was recovery.

Essentially no benefit from durations exceeding 4 hr per day and reduced benefits from practice exceeding 2 hr… the effective duration of deliberate practice may be closer to 1 hr per day.
— Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer, 1993

Now, let’s imagine this now.

  • You woke up. You doomscroll for 10 mins. Felt guilty. Open news, looking at stock prices, what Trump did, how many new features Claude shipped last night.
  • Information anxiety whispering into your ear that you are missing out.

That’s me, every day, for months now since AI is everywhere and you’re not cool if you don’t vibe code.

I get even more frustrated after my research, knowing that I get 4 hours of peak focus a day, and I probably burned 1 hour of it on X before 9am.

So I built my own source of information.

Pain

  • News has no “so-what.” I read newsletters, X accounts, 5 podcasts. My inbox is a graveyard of headlines, and none of them tell me what to do with the information.
  • Social media is full of noise.
  • I have a preference. I only want to see specific news about specific areas.

Solution

12:00 noon, daily. A pipeline kicks off on my Mac. End results are a web edition at GitHub Pages, a Telegram chatbot, and a two-host Mandarin podcast generated via NotebookLM.

What does what

Step 1 — Collect.

A Claude CLI agent with Gmail and WebSearch tools pulls raw material from subscribed newsletters and X. An aggregator, but only of my designated sources, ensuring no noise.

Step 2 — Interrogate.

Opus runs three personas in parallel: VC, Domain Expert, Founder. Each one generates the questions worth asking of today’s material. This is where the insight starts: we’re not summarizing yet, we’re surfacing what a smart room would argue about.

Step 3 — Self-Answer.

Opus runs two batches in parallel, answering each question and citing back to the Step 1 source material. Answers without source backing get cut. This step kills the model’s habit of default-to-grandiose.

Step 4 — Draft.

Reorganizes the Q&A into editorial articles with the three-zone structure: facts, “So What?”, “Worth asking.”

Step 5 — Render.

Produces the HTML.

Step 6 — Podcast.

NotebookLM automation creates a notebook, uploads the draft, configures a two-host Mandarin deep-dive persona, generates the audio.

Step 7 — Publish.

Other newsletters give you the headline and the summary. This one gives you what a VC, a domain expert, and a founder would argue about over coffee after reading the same headlines.

That’s the idea.

每個人都該有自己的一套資訊攝取系統，就像你的飲食一樣。

想像一下。

  • 你一天只能有 6,000 個念頭。連「晚餐想吃義大利麵」都算 1 個。
  • 你一天只有 4 小時處於巔峰狀態。一天剩下的時間，你只有巔峰時 30% 的銳利度。

這把我搞得很煩。

因為這不是想像。這是真的。

人的專注力有上限。

  • 你一天大約會冒出約 6,200 個獨立的念頭。（Tseng & Poppenk，Nature Communications，2020）
  • 就算是頂尖好手，認知表現的巔峰一天也只有約 4 小時封頂。超過之後，報酬急速下滑。（Ericsson，1993）
  • 專注工作 3 小時後，失誤率上升 71%、反應時間變慢約 5%。（Boksem et al.，2005）
  • 就算每 50 分鐘休息 10 分鐘，認知效率在休息 4.5 小時後仍無法完全恢復。（Brazaitis & Satas，2023）

狄更斯。海明威。史蒂芬・金。全都一天只寫 4 小時。剩下的時間是恢復。

每天超過 4 小時基本上沒有額外效益，超過 2 小時的練習效益也遞減…真正有效的刻意練習時間，可能更接近一天 1 小時。
— Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer, 1993

現在，我們來想像這個畫面。

  • 你醒來。先 doomscroll 滑了 10 分鐘。覺得有罪惡感。打開新聞，看看股價、Trump 又幹了什麼、Claude 昨晚又出了幾個新功能。
  • 資訊焦慮在你耳邊低語，說你正在錯過什麼。

這就是我，每天，已經好幾個月了——自從 AI 無所不在、不 vibe code 就不夠酷以來。

做完研究後我更挫折，因為我知道自己一天只有 4 小時的巔峰專注，而我大概在早上 9 點前就把其中 1 小時燒在 X 上了。

所以我打造了自己的資訊來源。

痛點

  • 新聞沒有「所以呢」。我讀 newsletter、X 帳號、5 個 podcast。我的收件匣是一座標題的墳場，沒有一則告訴我該拿這些資訊怎麼辦。
  • 社群媒體充滿雜訊。
  • 我有偏好。我只想看特定領域的特定新聞。

解法

每天中午 12:00，一條 pipeline 在我的 Mac 上啟動。最終成果是 GitHub Pages 上的網頁版、一個 Telegram 聊天機器人，以及一個透過 NotebookLM 生成的雙主持人中文 podcast。

各步驟在做什麼

第 1 步 — 收集（Collect）。

一個帶有 Gmail 和 WebSearch 工具的 Claude CLI agent，從訂閱的 newsletter 和 X 拉取原始素材。它是一個聚合器，但只聚合我指定的來源，確保沒有雜訊。

第 2 步 — 拷問（Interrogate）。

Opus 並行跑三個 persona：VC、領域專家、創辦人。每一個都針對今天的素材，生出值得追問的問題。洞見就從這裡開始：我們還沒在摘要，而是在挖出一群聰明人會爭論的點。

第 3 步 — 自答（Self-Answer）。

Opus 並行跑兩批，回答每一個問題，並引用回第 1 步的來源素材。沒有來源佐證的答案會被砍掉。這一步治掉了模型動不動就講大話的習慣。

第 4 步 — 起草（Draft）。

把 Q&A 重組成編輯型文章，採用三區塊結構：事實、「所以呢？」、「值得追問」。

第 5 步 — 渲染（Render）。

產出 HTML。

第 6 步 — Podcast。

NotebookLM 自動化建立一個 notebook、上傳草稿、設定一個雙主持人的中文深度對談 persona，生成音檔。

第 7 步 — 發布（Publish）。

其他 newsletter 給你的是標題和摘要。這一份給你的，是一位 VC、一位領域專家、一位創辦人在讀完同樣的標題後，會邊喝咖啡邊爭論的東西。

這就是核心想法。
