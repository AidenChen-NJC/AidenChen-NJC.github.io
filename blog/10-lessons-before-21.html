10 Lessons I've Learned Before Turning 21

Ten things I wish someone had handed me at 18. None of them are advice — patterns I only noticed after enough things broke.

  1. First principle → start with “why,” end with “so what.”
  2. Stop living in somebody else's box → find your passion.
  3. Define your success → stop chasing others' dreams.
  4. Burnouts are inevitable if you're not doing what you love.
  5. Anxiety mainly comes from not taking action.
  6. Remember why you started.
  7. You don't need to have everything figured out before you start → you will figure it out when you have to.
  8. Choose your environment → if you're the smartest person in the room, you're in the wrong room.
  9. There's no perfect first job; life is about pivoting, not about trying to make the right decisions — make the decisions right.
  10. Follow your heart → gut feelings are real.
